\subsection{Added baselines for the experiment in Section~\ref{subsec:main-simulations}}
\label{appsubsec:added-baselines}

Here, we compare GHCP with additional baseline methods under the simulation
settings of Section~\ref{subsec:main-simulations}. The data are generated
exactly as in the main simulation with $K=20$, $\gamma=5$, and $\alpha=0.1$.
We consider the fixed-size design of Figure~\ref{fig:N21-cov-width-20}
($N_j\equiv 21$) and the Poisson group-size design of
Figure~\ref{fig:poi-cov-width-20}
($N_j\overset{\mathrm{i.i.d.}}{\sim}\mathrm{Poi}(25)$). In both cases, we compare
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
    \item GHCP, using Algorithm~\ref{alg:dhcp} with the restricted donor rule
    $\eta=0.5$;
    \item HCP~\citep{lee2023distribution};
    \item Pooling CDFs, Subsampling Once, and Repeated Subsampling
    \citep{dunn2022distribution}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Fixed group sizes.}
Figure~\ref{fig:width-append-dhcp-fixed} shows the empirical coverage and width
across $B=1000$ simulation repetitions for the fixed-size setting, and
Table~\ref{tab:dhcp-hcp-alpha01-extra} gives the corresponding numerical
summary. The main qualitative message is the same as in the main text: GHCP
maintains coverage while its width decreases substantially as the initial
test-group sample size $o$ grows. The baseline procedures from
\citet{dunn2022distribution} are less conservative than HCP but do not use the
observed test-group data, so their widths remain essentially unchanged across
$o$.

\end{figure}

\paragraph{Poisson group sizes.}
We repeat the comparison under the Poisson group-size design of
Figure~\ref{fig:poi-cov-width-20}. Figure~\ref{fig:width-append-dhcp-poisson}
and Table~\ref{tab:dhcp-hcp-poisson-alpha01-extra} report the corresponding
coverage and width summaries. The qualitative pattern matches the fixed-size
case: GHCP reduces width as $o$ increases while maintaining coverage near the
nominal level, whereas the Dunn et al.\ baselines remain insensitive to $o$.
